Email support@neatspacecleaner.store with “Lumo support.” Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, Lumo version and what happened. Screenshots are optional; hide personal information first.

I already paid. How do I restore access?

Use the same Apple account used for the subscription, open Lumo's membership or Profile screen and choose Restore purchases. Restoration does not create a new subscription. Apple restoration covers access; local vocabulary and progress do not move to another device through this action.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Open Apple subscription settings to manage renewal. Uninstalling Lumo does not cancel billing. For refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem; Apple determines eligibility.

Which plan am I buying?

Lumo offers weekly and yearly memberships without a free trial. Check the actual price, currency and billing period shown by Apple before confirming. Any daily equivalent is an approximation; the full weekly or yearly total is charged, not a daily payment. Subscription renewal and cancellation are managed by Apple.

Why is a video or transcript unavailable?

The source creator or YouTube may withdraw or restrict playback. Some sources do not have an accepted Spanish transcript. Lumo does not replace unavailable speech with invented subtitles. Send the video's title or YouTube link to support so we can investigate.

Where is my learning data?

The default profile, saved vocabulary and progress are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app may remove them. Restoring a purchase does not restore local learning data. Lumo does not currently provide an iCloud learning-data account.

How do I change measurement or delete data?

Campaign measurement is optional in Profile and also requires Apple's tracking permission. See the privacy policy and data-request instructions for your controls and retained provider data.

Keep your credentials private.

Support will not ask for your Apple password, payment-card number or verification code. If we need purchase information, use the minimum receipt or transaction details needed to find the issue.
