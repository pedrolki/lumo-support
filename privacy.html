Privacy policy for Lumo Spanish, the iPhone app published by BNW STUDIOS. Contact us at support@neatspacecleaner.store.

At a glance

Your learning profile stays on your device. Apple handles subscriptions. Optional campaign measurement starts only after you allow it in Lumo and iOS. Watching YouTube videos also involves Google's services.

What stays on your iPhone

Lumo stores your profile preferences, Spanish level, saved vocabulary, review history, progress and cached lessons in local app storage. You do not need to create a Lumo email account. Your Apple account password and payment-card details are not provided to BNW STUDIOS.

Local learning data is not an iCloud account or a cross-device synchronization service. Apple purchase restoration restores subscription access; it does not restore local learning history. Device backups, if enabled in your Apple settings, are controlled by Apple and your settings.

Data used by connected services

Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat checks subscription access and restoration using an app customer identifier, purchase receipts or transaction identifiers, product and subscription status, dates, and device/app technical information. For a local profile, the RevenueCat SDK creates a per-installation customer identifier; this is pseudonymous, rather than a promise that no personal data is processed. Subscription events can reach Lumo's backend for reconciliation. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

Videos: YouTube and Google

Videos play through the official YouTube player. Connecting to or using that player allows Google/YouTube to receive technical data such as your IP address, device/browser information and playback activity, and to use cookies or similar technologies under their policies. YouTube may show advertising. Lumo does not download or rehost the videos. Google/YouTube processing is separate from Lumo's optional AppsFlyer measurement choice.

Content and backend: Supabase

Lumo uses Supabase to deliver lesson content and operate subscription and account services. Network requests involve IP addresses, request timing and technical logs. The current Lumo database is hosted in Ireland. If you already use a legacy signed-in Lumo account, its email/account identifier and synchronized learning data may also be stored there; this legacy path is separate from the default local profile.

Optional campaign measurement: AppsFlyer and Meta

If you enable campaign measurement in Lumo and grant Apple's tracking permission, AppsFlyer can process installation/session information, device and advertising identifiers (including IDFA when available), IP address, app/device technical information and events such as finishing onboarding, completing a clip or saving a word. These events do not include the saved word, transcript, email or profile preferences. RevenueCat can send subscription events and amounts to AppsFlyer when the permitted attribution identifier is linked.

AppsFlyer can share configured installation and learning events with Meta to measure Meta advertising. Lumo's current Meta mapping excludes event values and revenue. Personalized-advertising consent is not enabled by Lumo's measurement choice. Declining measurement does not restrict membership or learning features. Events from before consent are not replayed by Lumo.

Lesson preparation

Lesson preparation may use Kie/Gemini on authorized source content before publication. Tapping words, swiping videos and playback do not send a live AI prompt. Lumo does not send your local vocabulary or profile to this lesson-preparation service.

Support

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to handle your request. Do not send passwords, full card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information.

Your controls

  • Change campaign measurement in Lumo's Profile. Apple's tracking permission is also available in iOS Settings.
  • Withdrawing consent stops subsequent device measurement when Lumo applies the change. A closed app cannot immediately notify server services; previously collected data is not erased just by switching measurement off.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Deleting Lumo or requesting data deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.
  • Remove local app data by choosing Delete App in iOS, rather than Offload App. Manage any device backups separately in Apple settings.
  • For retained server/provider data or a legacy account, follow our data-request instructions.

Retention, rights and security

Local learning data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Purchase, technical, support and consent records may be retained by us or our providers for the purposes described here, including maintaining subscription access, responding to requests, preventing fraud and meeting legal obligations. Deletion may be limited where a record must be retained by law. Backup copies and provider records may be removed on a different schedule from live app data.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Email support@neatspacecleaner.store with “Lumo privacy request.” We may request limited information to verify that the relevant records belong to you. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

We use app/platform security controls and access restrictions; no service can promise perfect security. Providers may process data outside your country, including the United States. The applicable provider terms and data-processing arrangements govern their processing and international transfers. We use service providers to operate Lumo and require appropriate data protection in those arrangements. Our purposes are providing the requested service, handling support and security, complying with obligations, and optional measurement with your permission.

Audience and changes

Lumo is intended for adults and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact support. We may update this policy as the service changes; the revision date appears above, and material changes requiring new permission will be presented before the relevant optional processing starts.
