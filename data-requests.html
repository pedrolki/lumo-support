Local profile and learning history

The default Lumo profile is stored on your iPhone. To remove its local database, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Lumo → Delete App. Offload App preserves documents and data. Check your Apple backup settings separately if you also want to remove a device backup. Local learning history cannot be recovered through Apple purchase restoration.

Legacy signed-in account

If you have a legacy Lumo email account, the signed-in Profile screen offers account deletion with confirmation and recent authentication. This removes that Lumo account's backend learning records. If you cannot sign in or need help, contact support using the address below.

Server and provider records

To request access, correction, a copy or deletion of retained subscription, measurement, support or legacy-account data, email support@neatspacecleaner.store with “Lumo data request.” Describe the request and include the minimum information needed to locate the records, such as the email previously used for a legacy account or relevant transaction reference. Do not send passwords, verification codes or card details. We may ask for limited ownership verification before acting.

We will handle requests under applicable law and coordinate with relevant service providers where needed. Some purchase/fraud/legal records may need to be retained. Removing the app or withdrawing measurement permission does not automatically erase historical records held by providers.

Stop future campaign measurement

Turn campaign measurement off in Lumo's Profile, and manage Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings. The app applies the change when running. Server-side processing cannot be updated by an app that remains closed.

Data deletion does not cancel billing.

Cancel renewal separately through Apple subscription settings. Apple controls payment records and refund decisions.
